# Code of Conduct

TDOP is committed to a respectful, inclusive and constructive community. Participation in project spaces is expected to support collaboration across different backgrounds, languages, abilities and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate and constructive.
- Welcome questions and differing opinions; critique ideas, not people.
- Use inclusive language and respect names, pronouns and cultural differences.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Protect other people’s privacy; do not publish personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Deliberate disruption, sustained bad-faith conduct or abuse of project processes.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, project boards, online events and other spaces where a person represents TDOP. Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove content, restrict participation or take other proportionate action.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. At Phase 0, a dedicated private contact route has not yet been configured; maintainers must publish and test one before inviting external contributors. Do not place sensitive incident details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
